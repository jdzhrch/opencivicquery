# San Francisco request channels

This table lists where to send a public records request or a press question to 53 San Francisco agencies and offices: city and county departments, plus the Superior Court, the County Transportation Authority, the Housing Authority, the San Francisco Health Plan, and San Francisco State University. Every entry comes from the agency's own official page, named in the Source column.

## How to use this table

- **Use it when a record the reporter needs is not online,** or when a question needs the agency's answer. Give the channel together with the questions for the agency you have collected.
- **Give the reporter the channel, the source page, and the date checked.** Contact details change; the source page is how the reporter confirms one.
- **Role-based channels only.** The table lists portals, department mailboxes, and press lines. Where an agency's page names only a person, the table says "No role-based channel published" and gives the page; send the reporter there.
- **"Not found" means the official page did not label one,** or the page could not be opened. It does not mean the agency has no such channel.
- **Kinds of channel.** "Records request" means a request under the California Public Records Act or San Francisco's Sunshine Ordinance. "Press office" means media inquiries.
- **Three routes for records requests.** Many departments use the city's shared NextRequest portal (https://sanfrancisco.nextrequest.com/); the Police Department and the Sheriff's Office run their own portals; the rest take requests by email.
